## What is Educator Tools?

!!! note
    **Educator Tools is a free add-on for Minecraft Education Edition** that helps teachers manage their virtual classroom without needing to learn complex commands. Think of it as your digital teaching assistant!


🎮 **What it does:**
- Move students around the world instantly
- Get everyone's attention with one click
- Create timed activities and assignments
- Control the game environment (time, weather, rules)
- Build words and math problems with special letter blocks

---

## Why We Created Educator Tools

Minecraft Education is an amazing platform for learning and creativity. However, many teachers face challenges:

🚫 **Managing students in a virtual space** - Students wander off or get lost

🚫 **Getting everyone's attention** - Hard to pause the class when needed

🚫 **Setting up activities** - Complex commands required for simple tasks

🚫 **Creating educational displays** - Limited ways to build words or math problems

We created Educator Tools to solve these problems. Now teachers can:

✅ **Control the classroom easily** - Teleport, focus mode, timers, and more

✅ **Focus on teaching, not commands** - Simple menus instead of typing commands

✅ **Create engaging lessons** - Letter blocks, assignments, and structured activities

✅ **Spend less time managing, more time teaching**

---

## What Can You Do with Educator Tools?

### Classroom Management

- **Teleport students** to gather everyone or rescue lost students
- **Focus Mode** dims screens and shows your message for instant attention
- **Lock students** to specific areas during activities
- **Create teams** for group projects and easy targeting
- **Control inventories** to give materials or clear items

### Structured Activities

- **Timers** for timed challenges and activity transitions
- **Assignments** where students submit text and locations
- **Custom nicknames** for student personalization
- **Health management** to prevent interruptions from damage

### World Control

- **Set time and weather** instantly (make it always day!)
- **Toggle game rules** without commands
- **Block specific items** (like TNT or ender pearls)
- **Prevent certain creatures** from spawning

### Educational Displays

- **Letter Blocks** - Special blocks with letters, numbers, and symbols
- **Build words and sentences** for spelling and language arts
- **Create math equations** visually with number and operator blocks
- **Make signs and labels** for navigation and instruction

## Who Is Educator Tools For?

!!! tip
    ### 🎓 Teachers
    If you're teaching with Minecraft Education Edition, Educator Tools is for you! Whether you're:
    - Brand new to Minecraft or an experienced player
    - Teaching elementary, middle, or high school
    - Using Minecraft for any subject (language arts, math, science, history, etc.)

    **No technical expertise required!** The tools use simple menus - no commands to memorize.


!!! tip
    ### 👥 Students
    Students don't need to install anything. When they join your world, Educator Tools is automatically available. They'll experience:
    - Smooth classroom transitions (teleporting, focus mode, etc.)
    - Engaging activities (assignments, timers)
    - Educational tools (letter blocks for projects)


!!! tip
    ### 💻 Developers
    Educator Tools is open-source! If you're a developer, you can:
    - Contribute new features and improvements
    - Customize the pack for specific needs
    - Add translations for new languages
    - Create custom letter block sets

    See the [Developer Documentation](Developer-Documentation.md) to get started.
